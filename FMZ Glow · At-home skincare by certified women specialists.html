Good to know

Which areas do you cover?

Ahmedabad, Gandhinagar, Surat and Rajkot. Enter your pincode when booking and we’ll tell you straight away if a specialist covers your area.

Is there a travel charge?

No charge within 8 km of your specialist. If you’re further away, we tell you the exact amount on WhatsApp before you confirm.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes, free of charge up to 4 hours before your visit. Just reply to your confirmation message on WhatsApp.

Is it suitable for sensitive skin or acne?

Your specialist checks your skin first and adjusts or skips steps that aren’t right for you. For active skin conditions, please speak to a dermatologist first.

Can I have a facial during pregnancy or while breastfeeding?

Tell us when booking. The Galvanic Spa step is skipped during pregnancy, and your specialist will suggest a gentle alternative.

What do I need to prepare?

A comfortable place to lie down near a power socket, and clean, makeup-free skin if possible. Your specialist brings everything else.

How do I pay?

After your visit, by UPI or cash. You can also pay online when booking if you prefer.
