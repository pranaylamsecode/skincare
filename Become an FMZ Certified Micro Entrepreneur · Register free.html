Questions

Is registration free?

Yes. FMZ never charges a fee to register or to be selected. If anyone asks you for money in FMZ’s name, please report it to us.

Is this a job?

No. As a CME you run your own small service business, and FMZ supports you with training, a certificate, equipment finance, customers and the booking platform.

Do I need experience?

Not for Beauty & Wellness or Carer programmes; full training is given. Nursing services need a valid nursing or health registration.

Do I have to buy the equipment?

Selected CMEs can get equipment financed through FMZ’s bank and NBFC partners, subject to their approval. Instalments come out of your earnings on the platform. All terms are explained before you commit.

How much can I earn, and where?

It depends on your programme, area and how many visits you take. We share realistic figures, and the areas in the first intake, at the information meeting.
